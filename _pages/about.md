I am Xu Yang, a Lecturer in the School of Artificial Intelligence and Statistics at Xiangtan University.

Formal methods and heuristic search are often viewed as contrasting approaches. My research aims to bring them together, combining their complementary strengths to address their respective limitations and advance both fields.

## Research Interests

- Trustworthy AI
- Formal Methods
- Constraint Solving
- Evolutionary Computation
